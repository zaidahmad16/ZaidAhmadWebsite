Syllabus

A small desktop app that keeps my Brightspace deadlines, quizzes, exams and class times in Google Calendar, including dates that only show up in course announcements.

Python GTK4 libadwaita Google Calendar API Gemini API

What it does

Brightspace has a calendar feed, but it's noisy: every quiz shows up three times (opens, closes, due), course content "becomes available" every week, and checklists look like deadlines. Professors also announce test dates and extensions in announcements that never reach the calendar.

Syllabus reads the Brightspace calendar feed and each course's announcements feed, uses Gemini to separate real deadlines from the noise and to pull dates out of announcement text, and writes the result to a separate "Brightspace" calendar in Google Calendar, with reminders before each deadline. It also adds your weekly class timetable, skipping holidays and reading week.

Dates read from announcements are labelled "(from announcement)" and coloured differently, so they're never confused with official Brightspace dates.

Your data

Syllabus runs on your own computer. It only gets access to the calendar it creates in your Google account, not your other calendars or events. See the privacy policy for exactly what is sent where.
